Delete your CodeYatra account

CodeYatra, the coding app for students. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open the CodeYatra app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted from our servers straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email codeyatra.support@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email address
  • Your display name, bio and profile photo
  • Your synced learning progress: lessons, XP, coins, streak and settings
  • Your weekly league entry and class memberships (classes you created are deleted too)
  • Your certificates, so their verification links stop working
  • Your invite records, reports and block list, and Ask Yatri usage counts

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Anonymous crash reports and usage events never contained your name, email or account ID, so they cannot be traced back to you and are not deleted.

Progress saved on your phone stays there until you choose Profile → Reset progress or uninstall the app.
